# CoachVault Engine 3.2.5

Adds station vs live-play participation logic, outward queue direction, and fixed lacrosse field templates.

Station drills:
- waiting players outside the playable field
- first player nearest the field
- queue extends away from the field

Live-play drills:
- only active players shown
- waiting players assumed sideline or midfield

Half-field template:
- one end line
- one goal
- circular crease
- triangle goal
- restraining line
- no midfield circle
